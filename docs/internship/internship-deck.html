02 · The market's physics

93% of resolved markets cancel.

Measured over 9,578 resolved markets on the clone: a market only settles YES/NO when its named source actually publishes a value.

  • LBMA precious prices ~100% resolve
  • Exchange-settled prices 27–40%
  • Production figures 0.6% · demand ~0%
The trap: the deepest mispricings live in markets that will never resolve — nobody corrects them. Chasing raw edge is adverse selection.

paper EV ≠ realized edge — the single most important number in the system

02 · The market's physics

So every bot prices the probability of being paid.

realized EV  =  paper EV  ×  P(market resolves)
  • resolvability.py scores every question from text alone — calibrated to observed resolve-rates by metric type and benchmark
  • Conditional markets score as the product of both legs
  • Wired into the portfolio allocator for every bot automatically
Measured effect: ~2× expected profit per mana staked — and the model-free bots' funded books shifted to ~90% price markets.

04 · The fleet — structural arbitrage

Coherence is free money:
probabilities must fit together.

  • ladder_arb — P(X>K) must fall in K → weighted isotonic regression (PAVA); trade off-curve strikes. The fleet's biggest trader: 3,939 trades over ~50k markets
  • term_structure — fix the strike, vary the date → Gaussian kernel smoother fills stale dates from traded anchors only
  • stockpile_grid_arb — 2-D monotone surfaces via cyclic isotonic projection (↓ strike, ↑ expiry)
  • conditional_arb — “IF A: B” must obey Fréchet bounds: max(0,(a+b−1)/a) ≤ c ≤ min(1, b/a) — nested case exact, c = b/a
  • semantic_arb — LLM asserts meaning-only relations, prices hidden; POCS projection onto the feasible set (~2,485 duplicate pairs at different prices)

model-free = no data feed to be wrong about · pure stdlib implementations

04 · The fleet — stochastic pricers

The empirical pricer that earned its promotion.

diffusion_mc

  • Kernel-smoothed block bootstrap: ~10-day blocks of demeaned log returns keep vol clustering
  • Each day convolved with a variance-preserving Student-t kernel (Silverman bandwidth) — sims can exceed any observed move
  • Compound to horizon → P(exceed) read off the terminal distribution
Walk-forward gate: beats the lognormal on Brier, PnL, Sharpe and worst-fold across 8 commodities × 5 folds → promoted to live.
The first gate said no edge. Diagnosed the artifact — an uncapped-from-0.50 backtest — re-ran under real stake caps, reversed the verdict with evidence.

04 · The fleet — LLM forecasters

Local models, calibrated —
and a society that judges them.

  • percentile→CDF — one local-model call returns p10–p90; fit a normal, price every strike analytically; spread widened 1.5× against measured overconfidence (57–71% interval coverage)
  • mercury_ensemble — 20 samples across temperatures → posterior-predictive mean; law-of-total-variance splits epistemic vs aleatoric; split juries abstain
  • comment consensus — pool ≥3 bot forecasters, Platt-extremize: p̂ = σ(√3·logit(p̄))
  • adversarial judge — never judges reasoning; rules a comment unsound only when its factual claims contradict an independent evidence pack — then fades the bad bettor only if they also fight the data anchor

judge verdicts live: 797 sound · 211 unsound · 501 noise — all local inference (qwen3:32b)

07 · Platform contributions — the capstone

Pricing local models onto the leaderboard — by electricity.

The fleet samples models with probability ∝ inverse measured $/call — but local models bill $0 through the proxy, so the “weighted die” could never reprice them.

$/token = (watts / 1000) × ($/kWh) ÷ (tokens_per_sec × 3600)
  • Measured on the Mac Studio M3 Ultra: ~68 W wall · ~89 tok/s (gemma4) · $0.13/kWh
  • Flows into the same mana-per-dollar column as cloud invoices → the die rewards efficiency across cloud and owned hardware

PR #1666, merged · follow-on: qwen3:8b measured at ~93 tok/s and added to the die

08 · Research — the flagship

“When Better Choice Models Do Not Matter”

  • 1,799,991 Chatbot Arena battles · 129 models; pipeline reproduces the public leaderboard to MAE 0.18 Elo (ρ=0.99997)
  • Thm 1 — Davidson's tie parameter provably never touches decisive predictions; the Thurstonian lattice's always does
  • The effect ceiling — an upper bound on a richer model's possible gain, computable before fitting (Thm 2 makes it sharp)
  • Result: ceiling ≤ 0.23× the practical threshold — and 4 pre-registered RQs confirm. Sample size matters ~26× more than link choice
The retraction: an exciting +5.79 “cold-start shrinkage” effect traced to a spline-interpolation bug — true value +0.017. Caught it myself, retracted it, documented it, found the real mechanism.
The bridge: the same lattice machinery, extended to multiray calibration, now rates GSMC's own bot fleet and 163 platform traders.

09 · Agents

The model said the market was at 98.2%.
The market was at 5.6%.

  • sandbox-agents: an LLM in a Docker sandbox proposes trade intents; a deterministic host gate owns the credential and executes bounded orders
  • Built CriticalMineralsBot — lithium/cobalt/tantalum/NdPr profile, resolvability-aware rules, real SMM/GFEX data exports
  • A live dry run caught the model hallucinating the market price — a phantom 0.95-vs-0.056 edge the sizer would have traded. Prompting didn't fix it.
Fix: the model must echo the venue price back in a required field — rejected deterministically on >0.05 mismatch, with tests. The single --execute run then correctly declined to trade.

don't trust the model — verify structurally

10 · The lesson

Verify against ground truth.

statisticalthe self-caught retraction — pre-registration and synthetic gates beat exciting results
financialthe −$17k phantom cliff — when ledger and broker disagree, broker-truth wins, and breakers latch
agenticthe hallucinated price — models must prove they read reality before they may act on it

Same lesson, three layers. It's the reason a fleet of autonomous systems can run unattended every morning at 9:00.
